# 5. The "just this once" favour

**When to use:** A small ask framed as a one-off favour. Name it as goodwill once, or quote it — silent free work trains the next request.

**Subject (optional):** Re: [request] — goodwill or quote

```
Hi [Client],

Happy to help with [request]. It's outside what we agreed for this project.

Because it's a small one-off, I'm happy to include it as a goodwill add this time at no charge — on the understanding that anything similar going forward is quoted as a change order ([typical fee range] and [X days]).

If you'd rather keep the books clean now, I can do it for [price] with delivery by [date]. Either way works; just tell me which you prefer and I'll proceed.

[Your name]
```
